What the switch does

Off — quiet baseline
Keeps one baseline unit (DEMO20007 when available) and hides the demo notification and peer-comparison surfaces.
On — complete walkthrough
Shows the complete cross-unit scenario, a privacy-safe lifecycle spread (60% submitted and 10% complete), and all seven curated local notifications.
